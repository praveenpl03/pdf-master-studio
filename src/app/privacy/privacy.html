Privacy Policy

PDF Master Studio Privacy Commitment

Your documents, edits, and personal data belong strictly to you. We built PDF Master Studio with privacy at its core.

🔒 100% Local Processing

All PDF viewing, editing, rendering, and management operations happen completely offline and locally on your device. Your files are never transmitted, streamed, or uploaded to any cloud server or third-party infrastructure.

🚫 No Data Collection or Telemetry

We do not collect any personal information, usage metrics, crash logs, or analytics. Because the app runs locally as an open-source desktop utility, we have zero visibility into how you use the software or what files you open.

📖 Open Source Transparency

Because PDF Master Studio is fully open-source, you don't have to just take our word for it—you can independently audit the source code on GitHub to verify our privacy practices.
